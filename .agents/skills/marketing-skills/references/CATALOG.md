# The 50 Marketing Agent Skills

Use this catalogue as the single source of truth. Every skill has a fixed job and delivery contract. Where live research is needed, retain source URLs and label interpretation as interpretation.

## 1. Growth strategy and go-to-market

### 01. Market map
**Job:** Map a category before entering it. **Inputs:** product, geography, segment, date range. **Must return:** category definition, alternatives, demand drivers, market shifts, 5–10 named competitors, whitespace hypothesis, source list. **Checks:** separate direct competitors from substitutes; do not claim market size without a source.

### 02. ICP and segment prioritisation
**Job:** Choose the customer segment to pursue first. **Inputs:** product, customers, revenue goal, constraints. **Must return:** ranked segments, buying triggers, pains, buying committee, disqualifiers, channel reachability, scoring rationale. **Checks:** do not confuse a demographic description with a purchaseable segment.

### 03. Jobs-to-be-done interview synthesis
**Job:** Turn interviews, calls, reviews, or notes into demand insight. **Inputs:** transcripts or notes. **Must return:** functional, emotional, and social jobs; triggers; desired outcomes; anxieties; switching barriers; direct quotes with speaker/source labels. **Checks:** distinguish a repeated pattern from a single opinion.

### 04. Positioning brief
**Job:** Create a defensible market position. **Inputs:** product truth, ICP, alternatives, proof. **Must return:** positioning statement, category choice, differentiated mechanism, proof points, objections, message hierarchy, words to avoid. **Checks:** every differentiation claim needs product evidence.

### 05. Messaging house
**Job:** Convert positioning into language a team can use. **Inputs:** positioning brief, brand voice, audience. **Must return:** core promise, three supporting pillars, proof, objection response, homepage headline options, elevator pitch, CTA options. **Checks:** avoid interchangeable claims such as “all-in-one” or “best-in-class.”

### 06. Offer design
**Job:** Improve what a prospect receives and why they act now. **Inputs:** offer, price, margin constraints, audience objections. **Must return:** offer stack, value framing, risk reversal options, eligibility, urgency only when real, test matrix. **Checks:** no false scarcity, misleading discounts, or guarantees without approval.

### 07. GTM plan
**Job:** Turn a business goal into a focused 90-day route to market. **Inputs:** goal, budget, team, product maturity, channels. **Must return:** strategic bet, funnel model, channel plan, weekly milestones, owners, budget assumptions, KPI tree, risks. **Checks:** name what will *not* be done.

### 08. Launch plan
**Job:** Coordinate a product, feature, or brand launch. **Inputs:** launch date, product facts, audience, assets, constraints. **Must return:** launch narrative, audience sequence, asset checklist, channel calendar, approval gates, measurement plan, contingency plan. **Checks:** claims, screenshots, legal terms, and pricing must be verified before launch.

## 2. Market and competitor intelligence

### 09. Competitor strategy teardown
**Job:** Understand how a competitor wins attention and conversion. **Inputs:** competitor URLs, target market, date range. **Must return:** audience, positioning, offer, funnel, channel mix, proof, pricing signals, strengths, vulnerabilities, opportunity hypotheses, sources. **Checks:** cite public evidence; do not infer private performance as fact.

### 10. Competitor ad intelligence
**Job:** Analyse publicly visible paid creative without copying it. **Inputs:** competitors, platform, market, campaign goal. **Must return:** ad inventory table, hook patterns, offer patterns, proof types, landing-page match, creative angles, original test ideas. **Checks:** label ad longevity as a directional signal, not proof of return on spend.

### 11. Review and complaint mining
**Job:** Find what customers praise, regret, and want fixed. **Inputs:** review sources, product/category. **Must return:** theme frequency, representative excerpts, unmet needs, language to reuse ethically, product and content opportunities. **Checks:** retain source links; do not manufacture sentiment counts.

### 12. Social listening brief
**Job:** Track public conversations that affect positioning or content. **Inputs:** topic, audience, platforms, period. **Must return:** recurring questions, emerging language, creator/community context, sentiment caveats, content opportunities, watchlist. **Checks:** represent platforms accurately and do not treat a loud minority as the market.

### 13. Search demand landscape
**Job:** Understand what people search before creating SEO or paid-search work. **Inputs:** product, geography, seed terms, audience. **Must return:** intent clusters, SERP observations, content formats, commercial signals, priorities, keyword gaps, source/date notes. **Checks:** distinguish observed SERP results from estimated volume data.

### 14. Voice-of-customer library
**Job:** Build a reusable, searchable customer-language bank. **Inputs:** approved calls, surveys, reviews, support tickets. **Must return:** tagged quote library by job, pain, desired outcome, objection, trigger, and segment; three message implications. **Checks:** remove personal data and preserve context around quotes.

### 15. Category narrative tracker
**Job:** Detect shifts in how a market describes a problem. **Inputs:** category, sources, previous narrative if available. **Must return:** dominant narratives, new narratives, evidence, who is driving each, implications, watch signals. **Checks:** date-stamp every observation and avoid trend claims from one post.

### 16. Partner and creator fit scorecard
**Job:** Decide who is worth approaching for a partnership. **Inputs:** partner list, audience, campaign goal, budget. **Must return:** fit scorecard, audience overlap, credibility signals, collaboration concept, risks, outreach priority. **Checks:** follower count is not treated as reach or influence; verify public data.

## 3. SEO and technical SEO

### 17. Keyword-to-page map
**Job:** Assign search intent to the right page. **Inputs:** keyword set, existing URLs, conversion goals. **Must return:** cluster, intent, primary URL, supporting URLs, cannibalisation risks, recommended new pages, next actions. **Checks:** one primary intent per page unless evidence supports a broader page.

### 18. Content gap analysis
**Job:** Find the highest-value missing SEO coverage. **Inputs:** site, competitor set, audience, existing content. **Must return:** gap table with intent, business value, difficulty caveat, existing asset, recommended format, priority, source notes. **Checks:** a competitor’s page alone does not prove search demand.

### 19. SEO content brief
**Job:** Give a writer a useful, original brief. **Inputs:** target page, query cluster, audience, offer, brand sources. **Must return:** search intent, reader outcome, angle, outline, entities/topics to cover, proof needed, internal links, CTA, on-page checklist. **Checks:** do not prescribe keyword stuffing or copied competitor sections.

### 20. On-page optimisation
**Job:** Improve an existing page for clarity, relevance, and conversion. **Inputs:** URL or page copy, target intent, analytics if available. **Must return:** title/meta options, heading plan, content changes, internal-link ideas, schema opportunity, conversion fixes, change log. **Checks:** preserve accurate claims and flag anything needing subject-matter approval.

### 21. Technical SEO audit
**Job:** Prioritise technical barriers to crawl, indexation, and experience. **Inputs:** crawl export, Search Console data, site access or URLs. **Must return:** issue table with evidence, affected URLs, impact, confidence, effort, owner, fix and validation step. **Checks:** do not call a finding sitewide without representative evidence.

### 22. Crawl and indexation diagnosis
**Job:** Investigate why pages are missing, duplicated, or incorrectly indexed. **Inputs:** affected URLs, crawl data, headers, sitemap/robots/canonical data. **Must return:** diagnosis tree, probable cause, evidence, remediation, retest steps, escalation criteria. **Checks:** distinguish blocked crawling, index exclusion, canonicalisation, and rendering issues.

### 23. Structured-data planner
**Job:** Select and specify useful schema markup. **Inputs:** page type, visible content, business model. **Must return:** eligible schema types, required fields, content dependencies, implementation notes, validation steps. **Checks:** markup must match visible page content and must not fabricate reviews, offers, or events.

### 24. Link opportunity research
**Job:** Find relevant, ethical ways to earn links and mentions. **Inputs:** site, assets, niche, geography. **Must return:** prospect table, relevance reason, contact path, asset angle, outreach priority, disqualifiers. **Checks:** exclude paid-link schemes, spam networks, and irrelevant bulk outreach.

### 25. Topical authority plan
**Job:** Build a coherent content system around a commercial topic. **Inputs:** core offer, target market, existing pages, competitors. **Must return:** pillar-cluster map, internal-link plan, publishing sequence, expert/proof requirements, pruning or consolidation list, KPI. **Checks:** do not create thin pages just to cover keywords.

## 4. Content and conversion writing

### 26. Editorial strategy
**Job:** Plan content that supports a specific growth goal. **Inputs:** audience, product, funnel goal, capacity, channels. **Must return:** content pillars, audience questions, formats, distribution route, cadence, ownership, success metrics. **Checks:** every content stream must connect to a business outcome or learning goal.

### 27. Blog outline
**Job:** Build a clear, useful article before drafting. **Inputs:** topic, reader, search intent, point of view, proof. **Must return:** title options, one-sentence thesis, outline, evidence checklist, examples, CTA, internal links. **Checks:** the outline must answer the reader’s question, not merely repeat top-ranking headings.

### 28. Evidence-led blog draft
**Job:** Draft a useful article in the brand’s real voice. **Inputs:** approved outline, sources, voice guide, CTA. **Must return:** publication-ready draft, source links, title/meta options, image suggestions, editor queries. **Checks:** no invented experience, data, quotes, or citations; remove generic AI phrasing.

### 29. Landing-page conversion copy
**Job:** Write a page that clarifies value and moves a defined audience to act. **Inputs:** offer, audience, proof, CTA, objections, page constraints. **Must return:** section-by-section copy, headline options, CTA logic, proof placement, FAQ, test hypotheses. **Checks:** make the mechanism clear before adding persuasion.

### 30. Case-study builder
**Job:** Turn approved customer evidence into a credible case study. **Inputs:** customer approval, baseline, actions, results, quotes, assets. **Must return:** narrative, challenge, approach, result table, limitations, customer quote placement, CTA. **Checks:** use exact dates and measures; never imply causation beyond the evidence.

### 31. Comparison and alternative page
**Job:** Help buyers compare options honestly. **Inputs:** product facts, alternative, audience, evidence. **Must return:** comparison framework, fair strengths/limits, best-fit guidance, feature table, objection responses, CTA. **Checks:** avoid false claims about competitors; cite public product information.

### 32. Lead-magnet concept
**Job:** Design a resource people will exchange contact details for. **Inputs:** audience, problem, offer, lifecycle path. **Must return:** concept, promise, format, outline, opt-in copy, follow-up sequence, production scope, success metric. **Checks:** the asset must solve a real small problem, not disguise a sales brochure.

## 5. Social and community

### 33. Channel strategy
**Job:** Choose how a brand should show up on a social channel. **Inputs:** channel, audience, business goal, brand assets, capacity. **Must return:** channel role, content pillars, native formats, cadence, engagement practice, metrics, 30-day experiment. **Checks:** do not recycle the same post format across every platform.

### 34. Social content calendar
**Job:** Turn a strategy into an executable calendar. **Inputs:** channel strategy, campaign dates, assets, team capacity. **Must return:** dated calendar with topic, format, hook, CTA, asset owner, approval status, repurposing notes. **Checks:** balance promotional, proof, education, and conversation content.

### 35. LinkedIn thought-leadership post
**Job:** Write a credible professional post. **Inputs:** point of view, evidence, personal experience, audience, goal. **Must return:** 2–3 original draft options, opening line, body, CTA, visual idea, comment follow-up. **Checks:** no fake vulnerability, inflated claims, engagement bait, or invented anecdotes.

### 36. Short-form social script
**Job:** Write an original 15–60 second video script. **Inputs:** idea, audience, platform, proof, creator style. **Must return:** spoken hook, beat-by-beat script, visual proof cues, on-screen text, caption, CTA. **Checks:** the first line states a concrete payoff; do not imitate a creator’s exact wording.

### 37. Community reply and conversation plan
**Job:** Participate usefully in public conversations. **Inputs:** posts/comments, brand point of view, boundaries, goal. **Must return:** reply drafts, rationale, questions to ask, escalation notes, recurring insight log. **Checks:** add value before mentioning the brand; never use automation for deceptive engagement.

## 6. Lifecycle, outreach, and partnerships

### 38. Lifecycle email map
**Job:** Design email sequences around customer moments. **Inputs:** journey stages, product actions, data available, goals. **Must return:** trigger map, segments, sequence table, purpose per email, suppression rules, measurement plan. **Checks:** send only when consent and event data support it.

### 39. Newsletter edition
**Job:** Draft a newsletter people want to open and forward. **Inputs:** audience, topic, point of view, links, CTA, voice. **Must return:** subject options, preview text, structured draft, link context, CTA, send hypothesis. **Checks:** one clear editorial idea; never pad with generic summaries.

### 40. Cold-outreach sequence
**Job:** Create respectful, relevant B2B outreach. **Inputs:** ICP, public prospect context, offer, proof, sender identity. **Must return:** research note, email sequence, personalisation fields, opt-out language, follow-up logic, tracking plan. **Checks:** no fabricated familiarity or mass-spam copy; comply with applicable law and platform rules.

### 41. Partnership outreach brief
**Job:** Prepare a mutually useful partnership approach. **Inputs:** partner, audience overlap, asset, proposed value exchange. **Must return:** partnership thesis, tailored angle, outreach draft, activation ideas, approval list, success measure. **Checks:** explain why the partner benefits before discussing the brand.

### 42. Email deliverability diagnosis
**Job:** Troubleshoot falling inbox placement or engagement. **Inputs:** sending metrics, authentication status, list source, campaign history. **Must return:** likely causes, evidence gaps, remediation order, monitoring dashboard, escalation points. **Checks:** do not recommend purchased lists or deceptive domain rotation.

## 7. Visual and video production

### 43. Creative brief
**Job:** Translate a marketing idea into a brief a designer or creator can execute. **Inputs:** audience, objective, channel, message, brand assets, constraints. **Must return:** single-minded message, audience insight, mandatory proof, deliverables, references, guardrails, approval criteria. **Checks:** distinguish inspiration from material that may be copied.

### 44. Ad creative concept set
**Job:** Develop original paid-creative directions worth testing. **Inputs:** offer, audience, proof, channel, competitor patterns. **Must return:** 5 concepts with angle, hook, visual hierarchy, copy, proof, CTA, landing-page match, hypothesis. **Checks:** concepts must differ materially and avoid competitor lookalikes.

### 45. Video transcript-to-content system
**Job:** Turn an approved transcript into channel-native assets. **Inputs:** transcript, audience, channels, CTA, voice guide. **Must return:** key claims with timestamps, clip candidates, short posts, newsletter angle, blog outline, quote cards, distribution plan. **Checks:** preserve context and flag statements that need fact-checking.

### 46. Thumbnail and title pack
**Job:** Create clear packaging for a video or article. **Inputs:** topic, audience, platform, core payoff, visual assets. **Must return:** title options, 3 thumbnail concepts, mobile-readability notes, visual hierarchy, A/B test plan. **Checks:** no misleading framing or promises the content does not deliver.

## 8. Analytics, conversion, and client operations

### 47. Measurement and tracking plan
**Job:** Define what must be measured before a campaign launches. **Inputs:** goal, funnel, channels, analytics stack, privacy constraints. **Must return:** KPI tree, event taxonomy, source/medium rules, dashboard requirements, QA checklist, data owner. **Checks:** separate leading indicators from business outcomes and document consent requirements.

### 48. Performance narrative
**Job:** Explain marketing results so a decision-maker knows what to do next. **Inputs:** reporting period, targets, channel data, changes made, context. **Must return:** executive summary, metric table, drivers, evidence, wins/losses, next tests, decisions needed. **Checks:** label correlation versus causation and avoid vanity-metric conclusions.

### 49. CRO experiment brief
**Job:** Turn a conversion hypothesis into a test the team can run. **Inputs:** page/funnel, data, research, traffic, technical constraints. **Must return:** problem, hypothesis, variant, primary metric, guardrails, sample-size caveat, implementation notes, decision rule. **Checks:** change one principal idea at a time and do not call early noise a win.

### 50. Client communication and expectation plan
**Job:** Keep clients aligned before scope, performance, or timelines become a problem. **Inputs:** client goal, scope, data, risks, status, stakeholders. **Must return:** concise update, progress against plan, decisions needed, risks, next milestones, meeting agenda, escalation draft. **Checks:** state uncertainty plainly; never hide delays or promise outcomes that cannot be controlled.

## Standard result format

Unless a workflow calls for a different artefact, return work in this order:

1. **Recommendation:** the decision or draft in one paragraph.
2. **Evidence and assumptions:** source links, observed facts, and clearly labelled gaps.
3. **Deliverable:** the plan, copy, table, or brief requested above.
4. **Next move:** owner, deadline/trigger, success metric, and principal risk.
